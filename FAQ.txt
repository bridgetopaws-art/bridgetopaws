FAQ 
Section 1: About Our Products
Q: What is the philosophy behind BridgetoPaws products?
A: We create "healing memorial goods" designed to bridge the gap between you and your pet at the Rainbow Bridge. Unlike traditional somber funeral items, our products focus on beauty, art, and the celebration of the love you shared. They are symbolic messengers for your thoughts and wishes.

Q: How do I use the "Celestial Currency" and "Eternal Treats"?
A: These are designed for symbolic rituals. Many our customers choose to use them in a quiet moment of reflection, as part of a home memorial altar, or during a gentle ritual to "send" love to their pets. Our paper-based goods are crafted for elegance and a sense of ceremony.


Section 2: Shipping & Delivery 
Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship worldwide! We want every pet parent to be able to connect with their companions, no matter where they are.

Q: How long will it take for my order to arrive?
A: Orders are typically processed within 2-3 business days. International shipping usually takes 10-20 business days depending on the destination. You will receive a tracking number via email once your package is on its way.


Section 3: Payments & Security
Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We currently accept payments via PayPal, which supports major credit cards and ensures a secure transaction for our international customers.

Q: Is my personal information secure?
A: Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. Your privacy and trust are our top priorities.


Section 4: Returns & Support
Q: Can I return or exchange my items?
A: Due to the delicate and personal nature of our memorial products, we generally do not accept returns. However, if your order arrives damaged or there is an issue with the quality, please contact us at bridgetopaws@gmail.com within 7 days, and we will make it right.

Q: I am feeling overwhelmed by the loss of my pet. Can you help?
A: While we are not professional counselors, our brand was built on empathy. We hope our products bring you a sliver of peace. We often share healing stories and resources on our Instagram (@BridgetoPaws) and TikTok (@BridgetoPaws)—feel free to join our community.
